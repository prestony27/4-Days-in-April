# Masters Pool Rules:

1. A contestant chooses five golfers to form their team. From the World Golf Rankings, a contestant shall choose one (1) golfer from rankings 1-10, two (2) from 11-30, one (1) from 31-50, and one (1) from 51+. On the Monday before The Masters, the full field of golfers with updated WGR will be updated on the Pool website following the conclusion of this weekend's PGA tournament. **Rankings are locked as of 9:00 AM EST Monday, April 6th.** Submissions are not allowed before then, as the rankings will likely change from Sunday's tournament results.

2. The scores of a contestants five golfers are added together to form a team score. The lowest team score at the end of the tournament wins. If a contestant picks a golfer who misses the cut, the golfer's score simply will not change during the weekend (the golfer's score after two rounds is their final score). If any of a team's golfers DQs or withdraws after starting the tournament, the entire team is disqualified. Contestants choose at their own peril.

3. Each contestant entry is $30. Contestants may make THREE entries for a total of $90. If a contestant enters more than one team, no golfer can be on more than one of their teams. To be clear, a contestant cannot have Sam Burns (for example) on more than one team.

4. The pool website will re-rank teams on the website's Leaderboard as often as updated Masters scoring data is available.

5. Teams have to be submitted by 5 AM EST Thursday, April 9th.

6. Submissions are only valid once the $30 payment is received and a rule abiding team is picked.

7. **Pre-Tournament Withdrawals:** If a golfer withdraws before Round 1 begins, affected contestants may resubmit their team with a replacement golfer. If no resubmission is received before the deadline (or before Round 1 if after deadline), a full refund will be issued.

8. **Tiebreakers:** In the event of a tie for any prize position:
   - First tiebreaker: Team with the better (lower) score from their 51+ ranked golfer wins.
   - Second tiebreaker: Team with the better (lower) combined score from their two 11-30 ranked golfers wins.
   - If still tied: Tied teams split the combined prize money for the positions they occupy (e.g., tie for 1st splits 1st + 2nd place amounts).

9. **Prize Distribution:** Entry fees first offset operational costs (payment processing, hosting, data services). The remaining pool is distributed as prizes: 1st place (50%), 2nd place (30%), 3rd place (20%). Exact distribution may be adjusted based on total entries and will be announced before the tournament begins.
